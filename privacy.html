Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Aria is a private app used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app uses

  • A Google sign-in token with the single permission youtube.upload, stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions and tags that the owner approves for upload. Location and device metadata are removed from videos before upload.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone.
  • It does not read your YouTube data, other videos, comments, subscribers or account details.
  • It has no other users, analytics, cookies or advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

The sign-in can be revoked at any time in the Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
