Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Aria is a private app used only by its owner, with the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's own accounts (Google: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly; TikTok: user.info.basic, video.upload), stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions and tags that the owner approves for upload. Location and device metadata are removed from videos before upload.
  • The numbers of the owner's own posts and channel (views, likes, comments, watch time, audience totals), kept on the owner's computer for the owner's own statistics.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone.
  • It does not change or delete videos, comments, subscribers or account settings.
  • It has no other users, cookies or advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Sign-ins can be revoked at any time: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access; TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
