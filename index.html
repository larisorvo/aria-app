Aria

Aria is a private assistant run by one person for their own use. It publishes videos and posts to that person's own social media accounts, only after the account owner has approved each post, and reads the numbers of those posts so the owner can see how they did.

YouTube (Google)

Permissions: uploading videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload), and read-only access to that channel's videos and analytics (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly). The app never edits or deletes videos, comments or settings.

TikTok

Permissions: the owner's basic profile and public counts (user.info.basic, user.info.stats), a list of the owner's public videos with their numbers (video.list), and uploading a video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (video.upload), which the owner then finishes and publishes in the TikTok app.

The app is not offered to other users.
