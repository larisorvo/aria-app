Aria

Aria is a private assistant run by one person for their own use. With this app it uploads videos to that person's own YouTube channels, and only after the channel owner has approved each video, its title and its description.

The app asks Google for one permission only: uploading videos to the channel ("Manage your YouTube videos" / youtube.upload). It does not read, edit or delete other videos, comments or account data.

The app is not offered to other users.
